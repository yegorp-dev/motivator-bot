Motivator Bot

A Telegram bot that runs a friendly weekly fitness competition for a small private group of friends.

What it does

Participants connect their Garmin Connect accounts. The bot collects their workout and daily activity statistics, calculates points with its own scoring rules and posts the results to a private Telegram group chat several times a day. Seeing the standings in the chat keeps everyone motivated to compete.

  • Points for effort. Workouts earn points for the minutes spent in heart-rate zones; higher zones earn more. Everyday walking adds points from daily steps.
  • Daily summary. Every morning the bot posts the previous day's results and the weekly standings.
  • Weekly leaderboard. At the end of each week the bot announces the final standings and keeps a simple all-time record.
  • Weekly goals. Each participant can set a points target for the week and follow their progress.

How participants connect

Each participant connects their own account through Garmin's official authorization page. The bot never sees, asks for or stores a Garmin username or password. Access can be revoked at any time in Garmin Connect, which stops all data collection for that participant.

Who it is for

This is a private, non-commercial hobby project run by an individual developer for a group of about four friends. It has no ads, no payments and no public audience. Participation is by invitation only.
