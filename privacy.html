Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026

1. Who we are

Motivator Bot ("the bot") is a private, non-commercial hobby project run by Egor Podolyak, an individual based in Lithuania, who is the data controller for the data described here. Contact: podolyak.egor@gmail.com.

2. How you connect

You connect your Garmin Connect account yourself, through Garmin's official authorization page, where you choose to grant the bot access. The bot never sees, asks for or stores your Garmin username or password; it only receives an access token issued by Garmin. You can revoke access at any time in your Garmin Connect account settings.

3. What data we collect

From Garmin Connect, only from the moment you connect:

  • activity summaries: activity type, start time, duration;
  • heart-rate data recorded during activities;
  • for strength workouts: exercises, sets, repetitions and weights;
  • daily step totals;
  • your display name in Garmin Connect.

From Telegram: your Telegram user ID and first name, and the weekly goals you set with bot commands.

We do not request sleep, stress, women's health, location or any other data categories.

4. How we use it

The data is used only to run the competition: to calculate points with the bot's scoring rules and to show daily and weekly statistics, standings and goal progress. Heart rate is stored in a reduced form (time spent per 5-bpm range for each activity), which is what the scoring needs.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising or profiling, or use it to train, build or improve any artificial-intelligence or machine-learning model.

5. Who can see it

Your statistics and points are shown only in the private Telegram group of the competition, to the other participants. By connecting your account you agree that the other participants will see them.

We do not share your data with anyone else. The service relies on two providers to work: Railway (hosting of the bot and its database) and Telegram (delivery of messages to the group chat). They process data only to provide those services.

6. Legal basis

We process your data on the basis of your consent, given when you connect your Garmin account and join the competition. You may withdraw your consent at any time (see section 8).

7. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is connected. When you revoke access or ask us to delete your data, we stop collecting it and delete everything stored about you within 30 days.

8. Your rights

You can ask to access a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, receive it in a portable format, or object to or restrict its processing. Write to podolyak.egor@gmail.com. To stop data collection immediately, revoke the bot's access in Garmin Connect. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority, in Lithuania the State Data Protection Inspectorate (vdai.lrv.lt).

9. Security

Data is stored in a password-protected database, and administrative access to the service requires authentication. If a security breach affects your data, we will inform the affected participants and Garmin without undue delay.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date and tell the participants in the group chat.
